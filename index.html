Daily Food Plan to Excel

Upload the Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner images of the raw materials plan. Check what was read, fix anything highlighted, then download the workbook.

1. Upload images

```
Reading runs in your browser. The first run downloads the text-reading data, so it needs internet.
```

2. Check the readings

Your edits are saved in this browser automatically.

Yellow cells were hard to read or were adjusted. Compare them with the source image and edit; the highlight clears when you change the cell. Nothing is guessed for you: empty stays empty.

3. Download workbook

Sheets: BREAKFAST, LUNCH, DINNER, DAILY SUMMARY, RAW DATA (plus SOURCE SUBTOTALS if summary rows were found). Totals are formulas, so they update when you edit quantities in Excel.
